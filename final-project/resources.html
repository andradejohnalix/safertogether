Online safety

Build safer online habits.

A few changes to your settings and how you share can give you more control over your online space.

Check your privacy settings

Limit contact

Review who can message, tag, or mention you. Check whether strangers can add you to groups.

Protect your account

Use a strong, unique password and turn on two-step verification when available.

Share with care

Ask permission before posting someone’s photo. Keep addresses, contact details, and live locations private.
